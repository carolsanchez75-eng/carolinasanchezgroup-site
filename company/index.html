About the founder

Carolina O. Sanchez, Founder and Managing Member.

Carolina is a bilingual entrepreneur who has owned and operated traditional businesses and has trained more than 200 professionals. She founded Carolina Sanchez Group LLC to bring her business experience into digital marketing and artificial intelligence.

Education and credentials include medical interpreting studies at Harvard Extension School and a paralegal certification from Boston University.

The company serves clients in the United States and Latin America, in English and Spanish.
